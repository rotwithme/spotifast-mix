Spotifast Mix 0.14.5 puts local playlists alongside your Spotify playlists and checks for new releases throughout the day.

## New

- **Open local playlists from the regular sidebar.** Local playlists appear in the Playlists list with a Local label. Open or play them directly, search by name, pin them, arrange their order, or drop Spotify songs onto them. They remain accessible without a Spotify account.
- **Local playlists match the Spotify playlist layout.** The shared cover and title header, round Play control, and compact numbered song rows replace stacked forms and buttons. Playlist options and song editing live in menus, and rows support Ctrl/Shift selection and drag reordering.
- **Find updates sooner.** The app checks GitHub at startup and every two minutes while running, including when idle or in the tray. New stable releases use the existing update icon and Restart to update flow. Offline or metered connections can delay automatic checks.

## Fixed

- **Spotify songs in local playlists can load through your playback sign-in.** Metadata now uses the same account's existing Spotify session before falling back to the Web API. Duplicate songs retain their positions. Lookup failures explain sign-in, quota, or network problems, and the Refresh control retries the playlist.
- **Playlist edits keep song details visible.** Moving or removing a local playlist entry no longer clears the remaining songs' titles and artwork.
